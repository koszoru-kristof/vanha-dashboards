E-ink dashboard — design studies

Five layouts for a 7.5″ 800×480 panel, driven by the same device registry and mock feed as the 3D model. No grey fills anywhere — every area is black, white, or a 45° hatch of the two — so a 1-bit panel shows these as drawn. The only non-pure pixels are glyph and curve antialiasing, which the panel thresholds without losing anything.
